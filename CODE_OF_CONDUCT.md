# Code of Conduct

Go Study welcomes people who want to improve the project, documentation, accessibility, compatibility, or learning workflow.

Please:

- be respectful and specific;
- critique ideas and code, not people;
- assume good faith while still being clear about technical risk;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, spam, and abusive behavior;
- keep bug reports reproducible and discussions relevant.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive.
